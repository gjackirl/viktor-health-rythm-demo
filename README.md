# Viktor Health Rhythm — demo

A small demo showing how [Viktor](https://viktor.com), an AI coworker that lives in Slack, can help someone keep a gentle daily health rhythm. It doesn't enforce a strict regime.

> "The rhythm serves my life, my life doesn't serve the rhythm."

## The idea
Most habit trackers fail on logging friction rather than discipline. Here Slack is the interface: you mention how the day went in a channel, and Viktor does the bookkeeping.

- **Slack** for quick check-ins and gentle nudges
- **Notion** as the tracker (daily rows and weekly goals)
- **Google Calendar** for the daily rhythm and one-off appointments
- **A Viktor Space** as an optional dashboard

## Principles
- Gentle and never moralising. A missed day isn't a failure, and there's no "catching up".
- Low friction. One Slack message should be enough.
- Private by default. **This repo contains synthetic data only.**
